# Favorite Series: Peaky Blinders

**Peaky Blinders** is a British crime drama television series set in post-World War I Birmingham, England.

## Main Characters
- **Tommy Shelby** – The brilliant and ambitious leader of the Peaky Blinders.
- **Arthur Shelby** – Tommy's older brother, known for his temper and loyalty.
- **Polly Gray** – The matriarch of the Shelby family, sharp and strong-willed.

## Why I Like It
> *"You can change what you do, but you can’t change what you want."* – Tommy Shelby  
The show is known for its gritty storytelling, stylish cinematography, and strong character development.
